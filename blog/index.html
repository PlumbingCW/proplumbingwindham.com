Plumbing Tips and Guides for Windham, CT

Plain guides on the problems Windham homeowners ask about most, from drains and water heaters to sump pumps and boilers.

Written by the Windham Pro Plumbing team. Updated November 14, 2026.

According to Connecticut's 2022 State Building Code and Department of Public Health guidance, our guides cite the state sources that apply to each topic, so you can check them yourself.

  • Toilet fill valve repair

    A toilet that hisses, runs or refills slowly usually has a worn fill valve. Here is how the valve works, how to check it and what a Windham homeowner can do before calling.

  • Baking soda drain cleaning

    Baking soda is a safe, cheap first step for a slow sink. It has limits, and knowing them saves you time. Here is what it does, how to use it and when to stop.

  • Plumbing a bathroom sink

    A bathroom sink has three jobs for the pipes: bring water in, take wastewater out and let the drain breathe. Here is what each part does and where a project can go wrong.

  • Sewer inspection cost

    A sewer camera inspection has no single price, and the reasons why are useful to know. This guide explains what changes the cost and how to get the most out of a visit.

  • Replacing a water heater element

    When an electric water heater runs out of hot water fast, a failed element is a common cause. Here is how to test one and what the replacement involves.

  • Replacing a thermocouple

    A gas water heater that will not stay lit often has a worn thermocouple. This guide explains what the part does, how to confirm the fault and where the safe limits of DIY are.

  • Hybrid tankless water heaters

    The word hybrid means two different things in water heaters. This guide explains both, who each suits and what a cold-winter house in eastern Connecticut should consider.

  • Leaky shower faucet

    A shower that drips after you turn it off wastes water and often means a worn cartridge. Here is how a single-handle valve works and how the repair goes.

  • Burst pipe repair guide

    A burst pipe is a short, stressful emergency that you handle with a few clear steps. This guide covers what to do first, how repairs work and how to prevent the next one.

  • Sewer line leak repair cost

    Sewer repair prices swing widely, and the reasons are not hidden. This guide explains the factors that set the price so you can compare quotes with confidence.

  • Sump pump installation

    A sump pump keeps groundwater out of a basement, but only if it is set up properly. This guide walks through the pit, the pump, the discharge and the backup.

  • Basement crack repair

    Not every basement crack is the same. Some are cosmetic, some leak and some signal movement. This guide helps you tell which is which and what the repair looks like.

  • Does olive oil clog drains

    Olive oil is liquid at room temperature, which makes it feel harmless. It is not. Here is what happens to cooking oil in your pipes and what to do with it instead.

  • Installing a garbage disposal

    A single-bowl sink makes disposal installation simple, but the details still matter. Here is how the mount, drain and power connections go together.

  • Same-day boiler repair

    A boiler failure in January needs fast action. This guide covers quick checks you can do, what a same-day repair involves and how to keep it from happening again.
