Plumbing in Lebanon often starts with two things the town does not provide, a water source and a place for waste to go. Most homes here rely on a private well and a septic system, which changes how we diagnose almost every problem.

Septic.

Septic or sewer line: how we tell

If several fixtures drain slowly together, the cause may be the line, the tank or the field. We run a cable through the line from the house cleanout and see whether it clears. If it clears and returns fast, the tank may be full or the outlet baffle clogged.

We do not pump tanks, and we say so. If we think the tank is the issue, we tell you to have it pumped and inspected, and we come back for any plumbing work. Our sewer line cleaning page covers the line from house to tank in more detail.

Wells.

Pressure tanks and pump trouble

A well system adds parts that a city house does not have. The pump pushes water to a pressure tank, which holds a cushion of air so the pump does not run every time you open a tap. When the tank loses its air or the switch wears out, the pump starts cycling quickly.

You may hear it click on and off or see pressure swing at the shower. We check the tank pre-charge, the switch and the supply lines. A failed well pump itself is a job for a well specialist, and we tell you so.

According to Connecticut Department of Public Health guidance, a private well should be retested after work on the pump or pipes. If we replace a pressure tank or switch, ask for a water test afterward.

Before we arrive.

If a pipe bursts or a leak starts in Lebanon

In a rural house the first minutes matter more, because a well pump will keep pushing water until you stop it. Do these in order, and call us on the 24/7 line as soon as the water is under control.

Cut power to the pump

Switch off the well pump breaker. A pump left running feeds a leak without limit.

Close the main valve

Shut the valve after the pressure tank, then open a faucet to drain the line.

Protect the floor

Move boxes and rugs and use towels to stop water reaching the electrical panel.

Call us with the address

Give your road name and ZIP 06249 so we can find the house and plan the drive.

Housing.

Farmhouses, newer builds and the freeze risk

Lebanon has older farmhouses and newer houses on larger lots. The old ones may have galvanized or mixed supply pipes and uninsulated runs in outside walls. The newer ones tend to use PEX and have fewer leaks but more complex systems.

Long supply runs in cold weather are the main freeze risk. If a line freezes once, it is likely to again, so we mark the spot and suggest protection. See our burst pipe repair page and water heater repair page for the usual cold-weather calls.
