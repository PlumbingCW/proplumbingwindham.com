The area.

A small manufacturing village south of Willimantic

South Windham began as an industrial village. By 1833 it was forming around a factory that made papermaking machinery, set up in 1828, and it later had a sawmill, a felting factory, a gristmill and a railroad depot. Steep hills separate it from Lebanon to the west.

The village counted about 1,400 people at the 2010 census and spans roughly three square miles. The town plan describes a mix of residential, commercial and manufacturing use along Route 32, with higher-density housing next to Willimantic and low-density housing and farms elsewhere.

Water.

Water service and what it means for a call

The town plan mentions public water along Route 32 but does not map water or sewer for the rest of the village. That means two neighbors can be on different setups. A home on a main with a meter has a different set of checks from a home with a private well and a pressure tank.

When you call, tell us whether you pay a water bill or own a well. It shortens the diagnosis. See our water service line repair page if the problem is between the street and the house.

Seasonal.

Seasonal homes and cottages

A building that sits empty for part of the year needs a plan for the plumbing. At closing time, the water is shut off at the main, fixtures are drained and the water heater is emptied. At opening time, the system should be filled slowly and checked for drips before anyone leaves it unattended.

A burst line in an empty building can run for days before anyone sees it. If you open a seasonal place and find water where it should not be, close the main and call us. See our burst pipe repair page for what we do next.

Homes.

Year-round homes along the corridor

Most calls from year-round homes are ordinary ones. A water heater that has stopped heating, a drain that backs up after laundry or a toilet that will not stop running all come in the same way. We look at the fixture first and the building second.

For a tank that has stopped working, our water heater repair page explains how we decide between fixing it and replacing it. For a slow main, a sewer line cleaning visit clears the line.
