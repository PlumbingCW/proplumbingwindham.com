Water pouring in? Do this first

Four steps that limit damage until we arrive.

  1. Close the main water valve, usually near the front basement wall or the meter.
  2. Switch off power to any wet area at the breaker.
  3. Open the lowest faucet to drain the line, and move valuables off the floor.
  4. Call the line and tell us what you see.

When it cannot wait.

When you need an emergency plumber in Windham

A true plumbing emergency is any problem that damages the house, threatens safety or leaves you without heat or water. Our emergency plumber line is open 24/7, and we send a technician the same day when one is available.

Typical calls include a burst or frozen pipe, a failed water heater, a sewer backup and a boiler that quit in winter. If you smell gas, leave the building first and call the gas utility or 911.

Winter.

Cold weather and the 24/7 plumber calls we see

Eastern Connecticut winters bring frozen supply lines, boiler failures and water heaters that give out at the worst moment. Calls spike on the coldest nights, so early calls get seen first. A 24/7 emergency plumber matters most when the temperature drops after dark.

Keep the heat on, know where your main shutoff is and save our number in your phone. That one step turns a panic into a plan.

Cost.

How we price emergency plumbing work

You hear the price before work starts. It depends on the problem, the access, the parts and the time of day, and we explain each. Insurance may cover sudden damage like a burst pipe, but usually not wear, so ask your insurer early.

Connecticut licenses plumbers through the Department of Consumer Protection, and the standard advice is to check its license lookup before you hire anyone. Our work follows the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) that the state adopts through its 2022 State Building Code.

Many drain and fixture problems are fixed in one visit. For bigger jobs like camera inspections or repiping, we plan the work with you first.
