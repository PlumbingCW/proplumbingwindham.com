Commercial plumbing emergencies after hours

A burst pipe, a backed-up floor drain or a failed water heater in a business is urgent, and our 24/7 line handles those calls at any hour. Shut off the main water valve, keep customers and staff away from wet floors and switch off power to wet areas.

Tell us the building type, the floor and what is affected. If it is a shared building, tell us who else is on the line, since a backup can affect other tenants.

Drains.

Drains, grease and backups

Commercial drains see more traffic, and kitchens add grease. A regular cleaning schedule is cheaper than a mid-service backup. We clear lines with cable and jetting and check them with a camera when they keep clogging.

If your grease trap or interceptor needs attention, tell us. We explain what we handle and what a grease hauler handles. See our hydro jetting page and sewer cleaning page for the main line work.

Leaks.

Leaks in commercial buildings

Leaks in commercial buildings can be harder to find, since the plumbing runs through larger or multi-tenant spaces. We use meters, pressure tests and listening gear, and we isolate sections to narrow the search.

Commercial leak calls are often a long process of elimination, and we are honest about what is possible. Our leak detection page covers the tools in more detail.

Rentals.

Landlords and property managers

If you manage rental units, one clear contact makes repairs easier. Tell us the unit, the tenant contact and the access arrangements, and we coordinate with them. We can group visits to several units to save time.

We document what we find so you have a record for the lease, the insurer or the owner. For tenants, we explain what happened in plain terms.

Compliance.

Backflow and code items

Some commercial buildings need backflow preventers tested and maintained, especially where a lawn sprinkler, fire system or process equipment connects to the water supply. Requirements depend on the town and the water provider.

We tell you what we can do and what needs a certified tester. Asking early prevents a lapsed test from becoming a compliance problem.

Section 608 of the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) covers protection of the potable water supply, which is the basis for backflow prevention. We tell you which devices your building needs to test and when.
