Main line clogged: the first thing to do

A clogged main sewer line is an urgent plumbing problem, and our 24/7 line is open for it. Stop running water, do not flush toilets and keep the washer and dishwasher off. Water that cannot leave the house will go to the lowest drain.

If you can find your cleanout, a capped pipe near the foundation or in the basement, tell us where it is. It lets us start work faster.

Causes.

Why a main line clogs

The main line carries everything from the house, so it takes the load of every fixture. The usual causes are not mysterious.

Grease and wipes

Fats and so-called flushable wipes collect and form a mat that catches more waste.

Tree roots

Roots find small gaps at joints and grow into the line, narrowing it.

Scale and buildup

Older pipes narrow over decades until a small load blocks them.

Belly or sag

A low spot holds water and solids, so clogs form in the same place.

Method.

How we clean a main sewer line

We locate the cleanout, run a heavy-duty cable through the line and use a cutter head matched to the blockage. We feed cable until it flows freely, then flush the line with water to check that it runs clear.

Where a cable is not enough, we use a high-pressure jetter. We follow cleaning with a camera when the line is old, when it has clogged before or when you want to know the cause.

Decision.

Cleaning versus repair: how we tell the difference

A clean line that stays clean is a cleaning job. A line that clogs again within weeks has a cause, and cleaning is only a stop-gap. A camera shows whether the pipe has a break, belly or root entry that needs repair.

We explain what we saw before we recommend anything. If the pipe is damaged, our sewer line repair service covers the repair options. A camera inspection gives you footage if you want to see it yourself.

The IPC (2021 edition, adopted in Connecticut) calls for cleanouts at set points on building drains and sewers, which is how we reach the line. If yours is missing or buried, we may suggest restoring one.

Septic.

Septic systems and main line cleaning

Homes in Lebanon, Columbia and parts of Coventry and Windham are on septic systems, and a slow main drain there may not be a pipe issue. A full tank, a clogged baffle or a failing leach field can look the same from inside.

We clear and check the line from the house to the tank. If the tank is full, we tell you it needs pumping, and we can point you to the right trade. See our Lebanon service page for more on rural properties.

Local.

Older Windham lines that clog more often

In older parts of Willimantic and along Main Street, the mix of old pipe and mature trees makes repeat main line clogs common. A cleaning schedule can be cheaper than another emergency.

We note what we found each time so the next visit does not start from scratch. If a line clogs more than once in a year, ask us about a camera inspection and repair plan.

Common Questions.

Sewer line cleaning questions in Windham

Do you clean main sewer lines at night or on weekends in Windham?

Yes. The line is answered 24/7, and we send a technician the same day when one is available. Stop using water and tell us what is backing up.

What are the signs of a clogged main line?

Several fixtures back up at once, the lowest drain gurgles or overflows and toilets drain slowly. Those signs together point to the main.

How often should a main sewer line be cleaned?

Only as needed. If it clogs more than once in a year, the pipe probably has a defect and we recommend a camera inspection.

Can flushable wipes clog my sewer line?

Yes. Wipes do not break down like toilet paper and can form mats that catch grease and waste.

Do you clean lines that go to a septic tank?

We clean the line from the house to the tank. Pumping the tank itself is a separate service, and we tell you if it looks necessary.

What if the cleanout is hard to find?

We can often find the line through a toilet or roof vent. We can also talk about adding a cleanout so future visits are easier.

Will you check the line after cleaning?

Yes, when the line is old, has clogged before or the cause is unclear. A camera shows what the cleaning missed.
