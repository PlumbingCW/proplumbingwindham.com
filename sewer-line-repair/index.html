Sewer backup: what to do first

Sewage coming up a floor drain or toilet is a health issue as well as a plumbing one. Stop using water in the house, keep children and pets out of the area and do not run the washer or dishwasher. Our 24/7 line is open for exactly this call.

Do not use drain chemicals on a sewer backup, and do not clean sewage with bare hands. Open windows if odors are strong, and wait for us to confirm what needs to happen next.

Symptoms.

Signs the sewer line is damaged

Not every sewer problem starts with a flood. These are the earlier signs we hear about.

Several drains slow together

The main line is partly blocked or sagging.

Sewer odor in the yard or basement

A crack or open joint lets gas escape.

Wet or sunken spot in the yard

A leak in the line is saturating the soil above it.

Frequent backups after cleaning

The pipe is broken, offset or intruded by roots.

Detection.

Finding the break with a camera

We feed a camera on a cable through a cleanout or other opening and watch the pipe in real time. A locator on the camera head lets us mark the surface above the problem so we know how deep and where to dig.

Cracks, offsets, bellies and root intrusions show up on screen, and we save the footage so you can see what we saw. That record also helps if you are dealing with a seller, a buyer or an insurer.

Repairs.

Repair options: spot repair, lining or replacement

Which repair is right depends on what the camera shows. A short break may only need a section replaced. A long run of cracks may be better lined or replaced. A collapsed line needs digging.

Sewer repairs must meet the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) for pipe material, slope and joints, and some towns also ask for a permit and an inspection. We tell you what applies at your address before we dig.

Local.

Old pipe materials around Windham

Homes in older parts of Willimantic and Windham Center may have clay, cast iron or other legacy sewer materials, and these can crack, shift or collect roots. Newer neighborhoods may use plastic pipe, which fails in different ways such as joints pulling apart or a bellied section.

We confirm the material on camera before we guess. If your home is out toward Lebanon or Columbia on a septic system, the line to the tank is a different job and we check that first.

Order of work.

Cleaning first or repairing first

If the backup is a blockage and the pipe is intact, cleaning may be all that is needed. If a camera shows damage, cleaning alone gives only a short fix. We start with the option that stops the backup, then explain what the camera found.

Our sewer line cleaning service and jetting service cover the cleaning route, and we may recommend them first when the line is sound.

Common Questions.

Sewer line repair questions in Windham

Do you handle sewer line emergencies at night or on weekends in Windham?

Yes. Our 24/7 line is answered around the clock, and we send a technician the same day when one is available. Stop using water as soon as you see a backup.

How do you find where the sewer line is broken?

We use a camera on a cable and a locator that marks the spot on the surface. That tells us how deep and where to dig.

Will you have to dig up my whole yard?

Not always. A spot repair needs a small trench, and lining can avoid digging in some cases. We explain the plan before work starts.

How do I know if the problem is the sewer line and not a clog?

If a cable clears it and it returns quickly, the line may be damaged. A camera shows the difference.

Do you work on septic lines?

We can check and repair the line from the house to the tank. Tank pumping and septic system work is a different trade, and we say so if we think you need it.

Can you tell me the condition of my line before I buy a house?

Yes. A camera inspection gives you footage and a plain description of the pipe. Our camera inspection page covers what to ask for.

Who is responsible for the sewer line, me or the town?

It depends on where the problem is. The line on your property is usually yours, while the connection at the street may belong to the town. Call the town or ask us to help figure it out.
