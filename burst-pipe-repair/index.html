When a pipe bursts: the first five minutes

Close the main water shutoff valve right away. It is usually near where the water line enters the basement or on the wall by the meter. Our 24/7 line is open, so call as soon as the flow is stopped.

Then open the lowest faucet to drain the line, switch off power to any wet area at the breaker and move valuables out of the way. Do not touch outlets or appliances standing in water.

Causes.

Why pipes burst in northeastern Connecticut

Cold snaps are the main cause. When water freezes in a pipe, the ice does not break it directly. The pressure trapped between the ice and a closed faucet is what splits the pipe, usually at a weak spot or a joint.

Exterior walls, unheated crawlspaces, garages and basements with open windows are the usual locations. Corrosion and old joints add to the risk, and a pipe that held for years can still give way at a worn seam.

Frozen.

Frozen pipe that has not burst yet

If you have no water and a pipe is cold or frosted, it may be frozen. Keep the faucet open, warm the area gently with a hair dryer and never use an open flame. Shut off the main if you see any crack or bulge.

Call us before the thaw if you can. Pipes often burst when ice melts and water pushes through a damaged spot, and we can find the damage before it floods.

Repairs.

What we repair and how

The method depends on the pipe, and different materials fail in different ways.

Replacement pipe and fittings must be approved for drinking water under the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). We use approved materials and pressure test before any wall is closed.

Copper pipe

  • Cut out the split and sweat in a new section.
  • Press fittings work where soldering is risky.
  • Check for more splits nearby.

PEX and plastic

  • Replace the damaged run with fittings.
  • Use proper crimp or expansion tools.
  • Check connection points for stress.

Galvanized steel pipes are harder because corrosion weakens the whole run. If the pipe is brittle, a section repair may only buy time, and we explain when repiping is the better call. See our repiping service for that decision.

Other repairs.

Cracked, leaking and corroded pipe repair

Not every pipe repair is a burst. A pinhole in copper, a cracked drain pipe or a leaking fitting needs the same quick response, even if the water is not rushing. Small leaks do large damage over time.

For drain pipes, we replace the cracked section and test the repair. For copper supply lines, we use couplings or new lengths depending on access. We tell you what we find and what we did.

Local.

Protecting your Windham home next winter

Keep heat on, even in a seasonal home. Open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls during cold snaps and let a trickle run at the farthest faucet when temperatures plunge. Disconnect garden hoses and shut off exterior spigots.

Homes in Willimantic, Windham Center and rural areas near Columbia and Lebanon can see harsh cold. Tell us if a pipe has frozen before, because that location is likely to freeze again, and a leak check afterward is wise.

Common Questions.

Burst pipe questions from Windham homes

Do you repair burst pipes at night or on weekends in Windham?

Yes. The 24/7 line is answered around the clock, and we send a technician the same day when one is available. Shut off the main and call.

Where is my main water shutoff?

It is commonly in the basement near the front wall, near the water heater or by the meter. Find it before an emergency and make sure it turns.

Will my insurance cover a burst pipe?

Sudden bursts are often covered. Neglect, such as leaving the house unheated, may not be. Ask your insurer and keep photos and receipts.

How do I thaw a frozen pipe safely?

Open the faucet and warm the pipe gently with a hair dryer or warm towels. Never use an open flame. If you see a crack, shut off the main and call.

Can a burst copper pipe be repaired without replacing it all?

Often yes. A section can be cut out and replaced. If there are many splits or corrosion, we discuss replacing a longer run.

Do you do the wall and ceiling repair afterward?

We repair the plumbing. Drywall, paint and flooring are separate trades, and we can tell you what the repair needs so you can line them up.

How can I tell if a pipe in the wall has burst?

Look for water stains, sounds of running water with fixtures off or a meter that keeps moving. Shut off the main and call.
