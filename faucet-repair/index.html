What to do when a fixture leaks or a shutoff will not close

A supply line that has burst under a sink or a faucet that will not turn off is an urgent plumbing call, and our 24/7 line is open for it. Close the angle stop under the sink or the main water valve if the stop will not turn.

Place a bowl under the leak, clear the cabinet and wipe the area dry so you can see where the water is coming from. Tell us what you see when you call and we will tell you what to bring.

Faucets.

Faucet problems and their causes

Most faucet repairs trace to a worn part inside the handle or spout.

Drips from the spout

A worn cartridge, washer or seat.

Leaks around the handle

A worn O-ring or loose packing nut.

Low flow

A clogged aerator or mineral buildup in the cartridge.

Handle hard to turn

Mineral scale or a damaged cartridge.

Showers.

Shower and tub faucets that leak

A shower valve that drips after you turn it off, or a tub spout that keeps running, usually has a worn cartridge. In some bathrooms the valve sits in the wall behind a tile or panel, so access matters.

We identify the valve brand and replace the cartridge or the full trim. If the valve body is corroded, we explain the options, which may involve opening the wall. Single-handle shower faucets are a common repair and we carry common cartridges.

Supply lines.

Supply lines, angle stops and burst valves

The small braided lines under your sink and the shutoff valves they connect to are some of the most common failure points in a home. A burst supply line can release gallons per minute until the main is shut off.

We replace old lines, install new angle stops and check the connections. If the shutoff will not close, we replace it so you can shut off the fixture next time. Our burst pipe service handles larger breaks.

Decision.

Repair or replace the faucet

A faucet that is a few years old and leaking at the cartridge is worth repairing. One that is corroded, discontinued or loose in the sink is better replaced.

We can supply or install a faucet you have chosen. For bigger fixture or bath projects, our installation page has more detail.

The 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) caps flow for most faucets at about 2.2 gallons per minute and for showerheads at 2.5. A new fixture that seems weaker than the old one may simply meet the limit, and we can check that it is not a clog.

Common Questions.

Faucet and sink questions in Windham

Do you repair faucet and valve emergencies at night or on weekends in Windham?

Yes. The 24/7 line is answered around the clock, and we send a technician the same day when one is available. Close the shutoff or main first.

Why is my faucet dripping?

A worn washer, seat or cartridge is the usual cause. Replacing it stops the drip.

What if the shutoff valve under my sink will not turn?

Do not force it. Shut off the main valve and call. We replace failed shutoffs.

Can you fix a leaking shower faucet?

Yes. Most are a cartridge swap. We identify the valve and bring the right part.

Should I repair or replace an old faucet?

If it is corroded or parts are no longer made, replace it. If it is a newer faucet with a worn part, repair it.

Can you install a faucet I bought?

Yes. We install customer-supplied faucets and check the supply lines at the same time.
