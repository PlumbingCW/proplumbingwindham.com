Boiler Repair Same Day in Windham, CT

A boiler failure in January needs fast action. This guide covers quick checks you can do, what a same-day repair involves and how to keep it from happening again.

Written by the Windham Pro Plumbing team. Published November 14, 2026.

Start here.

Quick checks before you call

A few simple checks can save a visit or help us diagnose by phone. Look at the thermostat and make sure it is calling for heat and set above room temperature. Check the boiler power switch and the breaker.

Look at the pressure gauge. If it is far below the normal range printed on the boiler, the system may have lost water. If it is very high, the relief valve may be about to open. Do not keep resetting a boiler that locks out.

Causes.

Common reasons for no heat

Most no-heat calls fall into a few groups.

No ignition

A dirty flame sensor, failed igniter or safety limit.

No circulation

A failed circulator pump or stuck zone valve.

Low water or pressure

A leak, a failed fill valve or an expansion tank fault.

Control or thermostat fault

A failed control board or wiring issue.

The visit.

What same-day boiler repair involves

We start with a short phone triage to learn the fuel, the brand and the symptoms. On site, we check the call for heat, the controls, the burner and the circulators. We test the safety devices and look for leaks.

Many repairs, such as flame sensors, igniters and zone valves, can be finished on the first visit when the part is on the truck. If a part has to be ordered, we tell you the plan and any temporary steps to keep the house safe.

Windham note.

Boilers and the Connecticut mechanical code

According to the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code, effective October 1, 2022, the state adopts the 2021 International Mechanical Code (IMC), which covers boiler installation requirements such as venting, clearances and safety devices. A replacement or a new boiler may need a permit depending on the town.

If your boiler is old and the repair is large, ask about replacement options. Our boiler repair service explains how we decide.

Safety.

Staying safe while the heat is out

Use portable heaters only as the manufacturer directs, and never run a gas oven or a grill for heat. Open cabinet doors to let warm air reach pipes. If the house gets very cold, let a faucet drip to keep pipes moving.

If you smell gas, leave and call the gas utility or 911. A boiler that is out in a cold snap puts pipes at risk too, and our burst pipe page explains what to do if one splits.

Decision.

Repair or replace

An older boiler with a failing heat exchanger or repeated lockouts is a candidate for replacement. A newer boiler with a bad part is worth fixing.

We give you the numbers for each and let you decide. If a water heater is fed from the same system, ask about that at the same visit.

Your call.

What to tell us when you call

Give us the fuel type, the brand if you know it, the age of the boiler and what you see. Tell us whether it is a hot-water or steam system, which zones are cold and what the pressure gauge reads. Mention any error lights or noises.

If you have had a recent repair or service, tell us that too. The more we know, the better we can bring the right parts. A short phone triage often saves a second visit and gets heat back sooner.

Air in the system.

Bleeding air from radiators and baseboards

On many hot-water systems, trapped air in a radiator leaves it cold at the top. A bleed key opens a small valve so the air can escape. Do it with the heat on, and close the valve when water appears.

Check the boiler pressure after you bleed, since the loss of air can lower it. If the system needs more water, the fill valve should handle it. If pressure keeps falling, there is a leak and a plumber should look.

Prevention.

Avoiding a mid-winter failure

Have the boiler checked before the cold season. Watch for pressure loss, odd noises and uneven heat. Keep the area around the boiler clear and bleed radiators where the system allows.

In Windham, Columbia and other nearby towns, the coldest weeks bring a rush of calls. Early service is cheaper and easier than an emergency in January.

Common Questions.

Windham same-day boiler repair questions

Can you repair a boiler on the same day in Windham?

Often, when the part is on the truck. Same-day appointments are available when we can fit them in, and we tell you the plan on the call.

What should I check before I call?

The thermostat, the boiler switch and breaker, and the pressure gauge. Tell us what you find.

Why does my boiler lock out?

Ignition problems, low water and limit switches can cause lockouts. Repeated resets can hide the cause.

Is it safe to use space heaters?

Only as the maker directs, and never leave them unattended. Do not use a gas oven for heat.

How do I avoid a winter failure?

Service before the season and watch for pressure loss or new noises.
