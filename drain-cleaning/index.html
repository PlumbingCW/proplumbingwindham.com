A backed-up drain is an emergency when it will not stop rising

If water is rising in a sink, tub or floor drain, stop using every fixture in the house and call us. Our 24/7 line is open for backups, and we send a technician the same day when one is available.

Keep towels at the drain, move rugs and boxes off the floor and do not pour chemical drain cleaner into a standing blockage. The chemicals sit in the water and are harsh on pipes and on whoever has to work on the drain next.

Diagnosis.

One drain or all of them? That decides where the clog is

A slow kitchen sink alone usually means a local clog in the trap or the branch line. If the tub gurgles when the washer drains, or the basement floor drain bubbles, the blockage is further down.

When two or more fixtures back up together, the problem is almost always in the main drain. That is where a longer cable or a camera comes in, and it is the call we treat as urgent.

One sink slow

Grease or food in the trap or branch pipe. Often fixed with a cable at the cleanout or trap.

Tub or shower holding water

Hair and soap buildup, usually within a few feet of the drain opening.

Floor drain bubbling

The main drain is partly blocked or the line is overloaded after laundry.

Multiple fixtures backing up

A main line blockage or a collapsed section. Stop using water and call.

Kitchen.

Kitchen sinks: grease, food and the garbage disposal

Kitchen drains clog differently from bathrooms. Cooking grease cools and hardens on the pipe wall, and coffee grounds and food scraps catch on the buildup. A disposal pushes more of that downstream.

We clear the trap first, then run a cable through the branch line and flush it clean. If the drain slows again within weeks, a camera shows whether the pipe is scaled, offset or belly-shaped. Our disposal repair service handles the unit itself.

Methods.

Snaking, jetting and camera work: which tool and when

A cable or snake breaks through a blockage and pulls it out. It is the right tool for most clogs and most fixtures. For heavy grease, scale or tree roots, a cable can leave residue behind, and jetting or a different cutter is a better choice.

We use a camera when a drain keeps clogging, when the main line is involved or when a sale or purchase calls for knowing the pipe condition. Our hydro jetting service covers the high-pressure option in detail.

The 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), which Connecticut adopts through its 2022 State Building Code, requires a trap and a vent on every fixture drain and cleanouts at set points on building drains. Those cleanouts are our access points, and a missing one is the first upgrade we suggest.

Bathrooms.

Tubs and showers that drain slowly

Bathroom drains collect hair, soap and body oils. The clog usually sits a few feet down and is easy to clear. The key is clearing it fully instead of pushing the plug further along.

If a tub drain clogs often, we look at the overflow plate and the trap beneath it as well. Pulling hair out of the overflow channel is a common fix that many people miss.

Drain leaks.

When the drain line itself is leaking

A drain that leaks under a floor or inside a wall causes a different set of problems, such as stains, odors and damp insulation. A cable will not fix it, and we use a camera and dye or water testing to find the leak.

If we find a drain line leak, we explain the repair options before we start. Some are a small section swap and some are bigger, and we say which it is before work begins. Our sewer camera inspection gives a full record of the line.

Local.

Drains in older homes around Windham and nearby towns

Older homes in Willimantic may have cast iron or clay branch lines, and those can scale and shift over decades, which makes clogs return in the same spot. We keep notes on where the problem sits so the next visit starts there.

If you are in Columbia or Lebanon on a septic system, tell us. A slow drain there may not be a pipe clog, and the tank or leach field needs a different approach.

Common Questions.

Drain cleaning questions from Windham homes

Do you clear drains at night or on weekends in Windham?

Yes. The 24/7 line is answered around the clock, and we send a technician the same day when one is available. A backup from the main line gets treated as urgent.

Why does my kitchen sink keep clogging?

Grease builds up on the pipe wall and catches food, so the clog returns. A camera can show if the pipe is scaled, offset or sagging, and a good cleaning helps.

Is it safe to use chemical drain cleaner?

We advise against it. The chemicals sit in standing water, can harm pipes and are unsafe for whoever works on the drain next. A cable is the better first step.

How do I know if the main line is clogged?

Several fixtures back up together, the floor drain bubbles when the washer runs or toilets gurgle when you flush. That pattern points to the main line.

Can you clear a drain without damaging my pipes?

Yes, when the tool matches the pipe. We use smaller cutters for older pipes and a camera first when the line looks fragile.

Do you clean floor drains and laundry drains?

Yes. Basement floor drains and laundry stand pipes clog from lint, soap and sediment, and we clear them with the right cable.

Will you tell me if the pipe needs repair instead of cleaning?

Yes. If the camera shows a break, sag or collapse, we say so and explain the repair choices before any work begins.

What if the drain backs up again after you leave?

Call us. A repeat clog usually means the cause has not been fixed, and we look at the line with a camera to find it.
